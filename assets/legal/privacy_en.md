# Privacy policy

Kid Timer does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- **No network.** The app has no internet permission and never talks to a server.
- **No tracking.** There are no ads, analytics, crash reporters or accounts.
- **Images stay on the device.** Photos you pick for a timer are copied into the app's private
  storage and are never uploaded or shared.
- **Settings stay on the device.** Presets, preferences and the running timer are saved locally
  and removed when you uninstall the app.
- **External links.** The parent area can open the project page in your browser, only after you
  confirm. The browser then follows its own privacy rules.

Kid Timer is free and open source (Apache-2.0). Questions or concerns: open an issue at
https://github.com/masterkey-07/kid-timer-app/issues
